# Duke Farms Center for Conservation

Welcome to the public GitHub landing page for Duke Farms Center for Conservation.

This site highlights public repositories, data tools, and research resources supporting conservation science, GIS, ecological monitoring, and environmental data work at Duke Farms.

## Focus Areas

- GIS and spatial analysis
- Biodiversity monitoring

- Conservation technology
